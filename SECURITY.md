# Security policy

Please do not report a vulnerability in a public issue, discussion or pull request.

Send it to **security@supernovae.studio** with:

- a description of the vulnerability and where it lives;
- the steps to reproduce it, or a minimal proof of concept;
- the version, branch or commit where you observed it;
- your assessment of the impact.

You will get an acknowledgement within 72 hours and a first triage within 7 days.

This is the default policy of the repositories in `nika-open`. A repository's own `SECURITY.md`
takes precedence. The engine's policy, with its supported release lines and its defense layers,
is in [nika-open/nika](https://github.com/nika-open/nika/security/policy).
